# Research basis and design decisions

Checked 26 October 2026. Original educational adaptation, not an official CDC script.

## Curriculum evidence

- CDC Physics Syllabus, Forms 1–4 (2024), printed p. 2: https://library.cdcrepository.info/resource.php?download=1&id=237
- Ministry syllabus copy: https://www.edu.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/PHYSICS-SYLLABUS-O-LEVEL-FORM-1-4.pdf
- CDC Physics Teaching Module, Form 1 Term 1 (2025): https://www.edu.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Physics-Teaching-Module-Form-1Highridge-Feb-2025-1.pdf

The first safety competence requires practical performance. It includes recognising hazards, selecting protection, communicating advice and supervised emergency/first-aid practice. The lesson pack provides a study sequence and practical teaching stations for those areas; a browser score does not establish competence. Other Introduction to Physics subtopics remain separate.

The full syllabus was accessible. The Ministry module PDF timed out through search retrieval and returned HTTP 503 through direct download. Official indexed excerpts were inspected for tool role play (activity 1.1.1), hazard work, assessment and poster creation (activity 1.4). No full page-by-page module review is claimed. Remaining chapter details and local procedures require teacher verification.

## Website references

1. Oak National Academy: https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/lessons/hazard-symbols-and-equipment
Reviewed lesson outcome, key words, learning points, misconception, starter/exit assessment and resource organisation. Used structural principles only; the UK content is not treated as Zambia's curriculum.

Reviewed public page and visual appearance: generous space, clear typography, visual problems and prompt feedback. Used design principles only; no sign-in, paid lessons, proprietary assets or questions copied.
3. Additional checks: Brilliant educator page and learning-path guidance; Oak pupil materials guidance. These supported comparison of navigation and lesson organisation.

## Implementation decisions

- A reading-first page replaces the previous coloured-card and tabbed layout.
- Seven navigable sections remain visible and printable without locked progression.
- Interactive diagram, PPE matching, scenario feedback, editable poster and original knowledge questions supplement concise notes.
- Suggested three-period sequence is our editorial plan, not a CDC timetable mandate.
- Teacher stations address PPE handling, emergency drill and trained first-aid instruction. Performance is observed in person and revisited.
- Existing 200-pixel Canva preview is displayed no larger than native dimensions. Exact diagrams are original SVG. No private Drive content or links are published.

## Validation and limits

JavaScript syntax, IDs and anchors checked. Desktop browser checked hazard feedback, matching, poster editing, score, retry and review progress. Teacher content review, school-specific procedures, physical mobile/iPad tests and print-output inspection remain. No hosting or domain settings were changed.
