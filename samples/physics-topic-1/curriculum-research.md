# Research basis: Form 1 Physics Lesson 1

Reviewed 26 October 2026. This is an original educational adaptation, not an official prescribed script.

## Official evidence

1. Ministry of Education, 2023 Zambia Education Curriculum Framework: https://library.cdcrepository.info/general-resource.php?download=1&id=1
The framework places secondary education within the restructured system and provides the broad competence-based direction. PDF reviewed with visual checks where text extraction was corrupted. Do not treat this as a subject lesson plan.

2. CDC, Physics Syllabus, Secondary Education Ordinary Level Forms 1–4, 2024: https://www.edu.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/PHYSICS-SYLLABUS-O-LEVEL-FORM-1-4.pdf
Readable official repository copy: https://library.cdcrepository.info/resource.php?download=1&id=237
Reviewed introductory guidance and printed pages 2–3. The opening unit has five parts: safety, waste handling, apparatus use, foundational ideas and practical applications. The first listed competence concerns putting laboratory safety into practice. The unit therefore needs multiple learning experiences rather than one broad set of revision notes. Suggested activity categories include hazard recognition, protection, communication and supervised responses. Knowledge questions alone cannot demonstrate practical performance.

3. CDC, Physics Teaching Module, Form 1 Term 1, 2025: https://www.edu.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Physics-Teaching-Module-Form-1Highridge-Feb-2025-1.pdf
Official search-index excerpts of the introduction, usage guidance and first safety activity were readable. Direct full-PDF fetch timed out or returned 503. The excerpts support advance planning, active participation, varied methods, feedback and adaptation to learners; the initial safety activity uses safety-tool identification and enactment. Do not claim a full page-by-page review of this module.

4. Ministry announcement on implementation: https://www.edu.gov.zm/?p=6801 ; Parliamentary ministerial answer of 2 October 2025: https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/12604
The sources distinguish the new Form structure and phased implementation from older Grade 10–12 materials. Date labels 2023 (framework), 2024 (syllabus) and 2025 (teacher module/initial Form 1 rollout) refer to different documents/events, not conflicting curriculum versions.

## Editorial decisions

- Lesson 1 is a proposed 40-minute beginning to laboratory safety, not all of Topic 1.1 and not the complete safety programme.
- Short bullet notes define key words and connect each rule to a reason.
- Learners identify hazards, choose precautions, recognise PPE and role-play reporting a problem.
- Real hazards are replaced with pictures/cards. Emergency and first-aid practice stays under trained adult supervision.
- Teacher observation complements five original knowledge questions. Browser scores do not certify competence or represent official ECZ grading.
- Further safety drills and training continue after Lesson 1; waste, apparatus and other topics follow separately.
- Existing visuals are reused. The large hero is a high-resolution recreation of a Canva reference; the small safety scene is a Canva preview at native-supported size; scientific illustrations are SVG.
