## Curriculum evidence

- CDC Civic Education Syllabus, Ordinary Level Form 1–4 (2024), printed page 3. Competences: 1.1.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of Civic Education; 1.1.1.2 Engage in civic activities.
- Ministry catalogue: https://www.edu.gov.zm/?page_id=1142
- Official syllabus: https://www.edu.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/CIVIC-EDUCATION-SYLLABUS-SCIENCE-O-LEVEL-SYLLABUS-FORM-1-4.pdf (filename is misleading; cover and contents are Civic Education).
- CDC Form 1 Term 1 Teaching Module (2025), printed pages 9–13, concept, scope, values and participation. Readable copy: https://studylib.net/doc/28408386/civic-education-teaching-module-pdf

Seven original lesson sections cover meaning; knowledge/skills/values/participation; five civic dimensions; rights/responsibilities and scope; respectful decisions; participation; a practical plan/reflection. Illustrative scenarios and SVG are original Aplus work. The mock school election must not be represented as a national electoral procedure.

## Question provenance and limitations

All 16 questions in public/civic-questions.json are original syllabus-based practice, not transcribed ECZ past-paper questions. IDs civ-1.1-01 to civ-1.1-12 check competence 1.1.1.1. IDs civ-1.1-13 to civ-1.1-16 check understanding supporting competence 1.1.1.2; practical engagement requires a real activity and teacher/guardian discussion. A tick or multiple-choice result does not certify practical competence.

Drive revision collections inspected during research: 7. Civic education Questions Grade 10-12.pdf; CIVIC EDUCATIONS REV PAMPHLATE.pdf (2017); G12 CIV ED.pdf. These are older revision/notes, not authenticated dated national examination papers. No question from them is claimed as an official ECZ item. Legal facts and later topics must be checked against current primary sources before expansion.

Form 1 topics 1.2–1.10 are a syllabus directory only and explicitly unavailable. Keep actual topic names, clear source/version records and complete explanations when expanding. Do not imply full Form 1 coverage or CDC/ECZ endorsement.

Progress and activity plans remain in browser localStorage (aplus-civic-1.1-v1), independently of Physics. No server account synchronisation is claimed. An unavailable storage warning appears when saving fails. Offline assets use the existing network-first service worker cache.
