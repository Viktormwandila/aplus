What changes a pendulum’s period?

Change the length. Watch how the time for one complete swing changes.

Period: s

T=2πLg

Ideal simple pendulum, small angle, g = 9.81 m/s². Air resistance is ignored. The bob’s size is fixed for clarity.

Predict first: if the length becomes four times as large, the period becomes twice as large.
